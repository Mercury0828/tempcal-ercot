# Q3 (H3, comparison) — pre-registration v2 (definitions in prereg/COMMON.md)

**Methods (same test issue dates and horizons):** GUARDED on Chronos-2+T (main; fixed in advance — no test-based model
selection); the factorial cells (Chronos-2 load-only / load+T × S-CONF / TEMPCAL); B3 ACI (γ = 0.05) on Chronos-2+T;
B4 LightGBM + CQR and B4 + TEMPCAL-CQR; B5 ERCOT in-use forecast + S-CONF and + TEMPCAL (relative score; calibration
history from its own past postings); B6 seasonal naive + S-CONF.

**Eligibility:** before calculating errors or coverage, construct and save an outcome-blind eligibility mask keyed by
(series, issue_date, h) for each registered comparison; the mask of a comparison is the set of keys where every arm of
that comparison has a forecast; it is applied identically to those arms; every exclusion is reported by reason. Missing
forecasts are not silent row deletions, and paired calculations assert identical unique keys.

**Matching rule:** a width comparison between two methods in a cell counts as **matched** only if both meet the Q2
floor b_k in that cell **and** their coverages differ by ≤ 0.02; otherwise the cell is reported as unmatched with both
coverages. IS_0.1 is reported for every cell regardless of matching.

**Expectation (reported as data in both directions):** GUARDED is not worse than B6 in any matched cell; in the mild
bin its median width is within 20 % of B4 + CQR's; no direction is pre-written for the extreme bins or for B5.

**Fairness sentence (fixed, v2):** "Comparisons use the registered common evaluation rows. Study-controlled
weather-informed models and temperature-conditioned calibration use the same archived MOS source; B5's underlying
operational inputs are externally determined. Marginal calibration uses a 90-day window, TEMPCAL additionally uses the
registered historical and cross-series fallbacks, ACI adapts its nominal level, and GUARDED applies the A2 envelope.
Score definitions and calibration-history start dates differ as stated." Plus: B4 is tuned with 30 random-search trials
on 2020-06 – 2021-05 and its calibration history starts in June 2021; B5 is ERCOT's in-use forecast from the latest
posting at or before 06:00.
