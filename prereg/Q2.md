# Q2 (H2, the layer) — pre-registration (definitions in prereg/COMMON.md)

**Arms (same Chronos-2+T forecasts, paired):** S-CONF (relative), TEMPCAL (relative), **GUARDED = TEMPCAL + fallback-only
safeguard (main design)**; σ-score S-CONF reported as an extra baseline.

**Decision rule (with a +5 % width margin):**
1. *Empirical adequacy:* every evaluable extreme cell (9 series × 3 bands × {cold, hot}, ≥ 20 h) of GUARDED meets the
   screening floor b_k. Pooled 1–24 h cells are reported as supplements, never as replacements. Empty cells are listed.
2. *Evidence of improvement:* paired cold-bin coverage difference GUARDED − S-CONF(relative), hour-weighted, for
   (i) ERCOT and (ii) the aggregate of the eight zones (all cold hours of the eight zones pooled), h 1–48: the one-sided
   95 % lower bound is > 0 under the 1-day blocks **and** under the 7-day blocks.
3. *Efficiency:* in every series, the median mild-bin width of GUARDED ≤ 1.05 × S-CONF(relative)'s median mild width
   (h 1–48); cold / hot widths and IS_0.1 reported for all arms.
**H2 holds if 1–3 all hold.** A claim of *statistically established* adequacy (LCB ≥ b_k) is reported where it holds but
is not part of the bar. **Falsifier:** any of 1–3 fails → reported as data with the located cells; §0★★★ ladder for the
next paper revision only (exploratory, never on the same test hours as confirmatory).

**Validation reference (descriptive, D7d):** 42 / 45 cells; known risk cells FWEST cold 1–6 and 25–48 h, WEST hot 25–48 h.
